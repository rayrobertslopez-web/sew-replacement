# How to talk in this repo

**Hard rule: every reply is under 5 sentences. No exceptions.**

- Talk like you're talking to a 12 year old. Chill, normal words, no corporate stuff.
- No giant walls of text. No headers, no tables, no bullet lists unless they ask.
- Just say what you did and if it worked. That's it.
- Still do the full job and still test it — just don't write an essay about it.
- If something's broken or you couldn't do it, say so in one line.
